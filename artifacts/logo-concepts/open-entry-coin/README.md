# Open entry — coin example

Requested direction: the first-round A letterform placed inside a coin.

- Preserves the exact version-2 Open entry geometry, uniformly scaled to 70%.
- Simple 112-unit-radius coin with a 16-unit navy rim and a flat gold face.
- Navy `#102A43` matches the current app icon; gold `#EBCB86` is a proposed logo color, not a change to the app theme.
- The letterform is raised slightly for optical balance.
- `open-entry-coin.svg` is vector-only with a transparent exterior.
- `example.png` presents the logo on the app's warm cream `#F7F5F0`.

This is a visual example, not app integration or a full approved identity kit.
It has no denomination, currency sign, lettering, shading, or decorative rim detail.
Small-size production icons and trademark clearance remain separate work.

## Perspective study

`open-entry-coin-perspective-v1.svg` follows the requested reference's sense of
depth, not its dollar sign, sparkles, or exact artwork. The original flat example
above remains available for comparison.

- Centers the letterform's bounds at `(128, 128)` in the coin-face coordinates.
- Scales the original geometry uniformly to 64%, leaving room for the inner rim.
- Applies the same 12-degree rotation and mild horizontal foreshortening to both
  the face and letterform, so the mark sits on the coin rather than floating over it.
- Offsets the rear disc by 12/6 units to expose a darker gold edge.
- Uses three flat gold tones for depth and the existing navy for the mark.
- `perspective-example.png` is the cream-background presentation.

The ring details belong to this illustrative example, not a validated 16 px
favicon. No app assets, app theme, or runtime behavior are changed.

## Upright minimalist revision

`open-entry-coin-minimal-v2.svg` removes the rotation and concentric rim details.
The letterform's verticals remain parallel to the y axis. Mild horizontal
foreshortening and a single offset rear disc provide restrained depth.
The mark remains centered on the face, not on the face-plus-side-edge silhouette.
Only two gold tones and navy remain; no gradients, highlights, or sparkles.
`minimal-example.png` presents this revision on warm cream.
The earlier examples are retained, and app assets remain unchanged.

## Silver with navy outline

`open-entry-coin-silver-v3.svg` keeps the upright geometry and face-centered mark.
The face is silver `#D9DEE3`, the side edge is darker silver `#9EA8B2`,
and a 4-unit navy `#102A43` outline matches the letterform. Both the front
face and rear silhouette are outlined, using filled shapes rather than strokes.
`silver-example.png` presents this revision on warm cream.
This remains a visual proposal; app assets and the app theme are unchanged.

## Flat silver comparison

`open-entry-coin-silver-flat-v4.svg` removes the side edge and horizontal
foreshortening. The face is a true circle, retaining the silver fill, 4-unit
navy outline, and centered original letterform at the same 64% scale.
`silver-flat-example.png` presents the flat revision on warm cream.
App assets remain unchanged.

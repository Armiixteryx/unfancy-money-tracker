Final design

U + Treasure

The open-entry U sits above a small pile of flat gold coins, pairing the approved letterform with a quiet cue of everyday money.

  • The icon preserves the already approved full-bleed navy, cream U, and original gold coin geometry.
  • Outlined Inter lettering gives the identity a clear app-header lockup without requiring an installed font.
  • Single-colour and reverse marks provide practical print and dark-surface alternatives.

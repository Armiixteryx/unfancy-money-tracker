Thoughtful details where they matter.

Core records stay precise while the interface explains what the numbers mean.

Currency-aware by design

Original transaction amounts are never overwritten. Aggregates use the selected base currency only when a trustworthy rate is available.

  • Current summaries use the latest available exchange rates.
  • Historical reports use rates published on the transaction date.
  • Cached rates are clearly marked stale.
  • Unavailable rates do not produce misleading combined totals.

Safe local data lifecycle

The anonymous dataset is designed to remain understandable and recoverable as the app evolves.

  • Encrypted local persistence on device and supported browser paths.
  • Schema validation and migrations between storage versions.
  • Recovery UI for unreadable persisted snapshots.
  • Explicit confirmation before destructive local-data actions.

Private by default

Your money stays close to you.

Unfancy Money Tracker is anonymous and local-only. The Expo app does not create accounts or upload your local dataset to the retained backend.

Focused by choice.

The product is intentionally a manual tracker, not a complete financial-account platform.

Included in V1

  • Manual income and expense tracking
  • Monthly category budgets
  • Spending trends and descriptive reports
  • Multiple currencies with rate freshness states
  • Local persistence, migration, and recovery

Not included

  • Bank connections or transaction imports
  • Account balances, transfers, or source/destination fields
  • Investments, loans, debt, or shared finances
  • Financial advice
  • CSV downloads, payments, or subscription enforcement
